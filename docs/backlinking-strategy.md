## 🔒 LAMH SAFETY PROTOCOL (overrides everything below)

**lametrohomefinder.com is TAE's crown-jewel asset — protect its authority at all costs.**

Google's Penguin algorithm specifically penalizes sites that over-link to owned properties ("link schemes"). Losing LAMH's 1.14M impressions would be catastrophic. Rules:

1. **Max 1 outbound link per new LAMH post** to another portfolio site, and ONLY when contextually unavoidable.
2. **Existing LAMH content is off-limits** — never go back and insert cross-links into existing posts that rank.
3. **No sidebar/footer links** from LAMH to other portfolio sites. Ever.
4. **No exact-match anchor text** — always use natural phrasing ("our partner" / "a team I trust" / "see also") rather than keyword-rich anchors.
5. **Editorial-only.** If the link wouldn't exist on a neutral 3rd-party publication covering the same topic, it doesn't belong.
6. **Rate limit across portfolio.** LAMH gets max 2 outbound portfolio-links in any 90-day window, spread apart.
7. **Audit LAMH's outbound-link ratio monthly.** If portfolio-owned links exceed 5% of total outbounds, freeze all new placements.

**If a placement feels borderline, it's out. We'd rather under-optimize than put 1.14M impressions at risk.**
